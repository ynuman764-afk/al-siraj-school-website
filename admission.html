Step-By-Step

Admission Procedure

1

Registration & Form Submission

Fill out our online admission form below or visit the campus admission counter to collect the prospectus and registration pack.

2

Diagnostic Assessment / Interview

For Class 1 to 9th, a brief baseline test in English, Math, and Urdu is conducted to determine the student's foundation level.

3

Offer of Admission & Fee Challan

Successful applicants receive an admission offer letter along with the bank fee voucher for fee deposit.

4

Orientation & Class Commencement

Students receive their uniform badge, syllabus booklist, and orientation schedule to begin classes.

Checklist

Required Documents

Please prepare the following documents when finalizing the admission dossier:

  • NADRA Form-B / Official Birth Certificate
    Attested photocopy of student's birth certificate or NADRA B-Form.
  • Father / Guardian CNIC Photocopy
    Clear photocopy of the parent's Computerized National Identity Card.
  • Four (4) Passport Size Photographs
    Recent blue-background passport photographs of the student.
  • Previous School Leaving Certificate (SLC)
    Original transfer/leaving certificate from previous school (Class 1st and above).
  • Previous Year Progress Report Card
    Photocopy of recent examination marksheet or progress record.
