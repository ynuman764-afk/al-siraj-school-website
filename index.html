Welcome To Our Institution

Welcome to AL-SIRAJ MODEL HIGH SCHOOL

AL-SIRAJ MODEL HIGH SCHOOL (Al-Siraj Model Boys High School, New Campus) stands as a landmark of educational progress on Shahbazpur Road. Spread across a sprawling 11-kanal purpose-built campus, our institution integrates rigorous scholastic standards with Islamic ethical values.

We believe that true education enlightens the mind ("Al-Siraj" – The Illuminating Lamp) while grounding students in discipline, empathy, and intellectual curiosity. From Play Group through 9th Class and our prestigious Hifz-ul-Quran division, every student receives attentive, personalized mentoring in an atmosphere of warmth and high expectations.

  • Modern 11-Kanal spacious campus with lush green lawns and sports fields.
  • Dedicated Science, Physics, Chemistry, and IT Computer Laboratories.
  • Synchronized Hifz-ul-Quran curriculum parallel with regular schooling.
