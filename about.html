Our Heritage & Foundation

The Story of Al-Siraj

Established with the noble objective of illuminating young minds, AL-SIRAJ MODEL HIGH SCHOOL draws its name from the timeless Arabic word Al-Siraj — the radiant beacon of guidance. From our modest beginnings, the institution has blossomed into a premier seat of learning.

Responding to the growing need for expansive, healthy spaces for youth, the school inaugurated its flagship 11-Kanal New Campus on Shahbazpur Road. This purpose-built campus provides students with spacious classrooms, dedicated science and computer laboratories, and vast sports fields that nurture intellectual curiosity and physical vitality alike.

Today, Al-Siraj stands as a symbol of educational reform, proving that international pedagogical standards and deep-rooted Islamic values can coalesce seamlessly into an uplifting schooling experience.
